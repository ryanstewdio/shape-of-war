# Hand off: foundry trough
Branch feat/foundry-trough from main 09b46f9. New GenConfig.enableTrough (default on) and GENERATOR_VERSION_V12, on trough levels only; V11_GEN_CONFIG turns troughs off. Versions 1 to 11 are bit identical: V11_GEN_CONFIG matches main's digest 35821CFF over 720 levels, and every non trough level with the flag on equals version 11.
- Choice (generator isTroughLevel): a level from 24 that is not a boss level and not a drum is a candidate when one draw on its own stream (TROUGH_SALT) is under 0.24. In a run of back to back candidates only every other level is a trough, so there are never two in a row. Measured share: 20.1 percent of eligible levels (400 seeds).
- Trough levels, 24 to 60: seed 42 gets 24, 32, 38, 42, 44, 47, 54; seed 2471588027 gets 26, 58.
- Maps (src/levelgen/trough.ts): rect or rectObstacles, aspect at least 1.5 (a wider floor), at most 2 obstacles with every corner within |x| 0.55 of the half width. Fixed spawn points are pulled in by x times 0.75, so all of them sit within |x| 0.75 (about 0.1 percent fall back to a ring). At most 1 well, placed within |x| 0.55. Spawners stay within |x| 0.75. Par uses the existing formula.
- Validator: V1 accepts 'trough' unless opts.enableTrough is false. TR1 checks level, non boss and V12 both ways, plus the aspect. TR2 checks the map rules. J3 knows 12, and J13 accepts 11 or 12. Free Play adds FP7, FP3 checks options.surface, and FP4 caps trough wells at 1.
- Free Play: FreePlayConfig.surface 'trough' builds a rect trough of the chosen size at version 12, whatever arena was picked (freePlay.arena records 'rect'). Barriers are 2 obstacles on the floor, there is at most 1 well, and segment spawn points are pulled in. Flat or absent surface gives the same bytes as before.
- Tests: tests/levelgen/trough.test.ts adds 18 tests (helpers, pinned trough lists, share and rules, frozen digest, maps over 120 seeds with 0 retries, par, TR1, TR2, V1, Free Play). Pins moved: boss.test and v2enemies.test now use 13 as the first unknown version, because 12 is now real. tests/qa did not move.
- npm test: 5056 passed, 1 failed. The failure is the accepted perf test "200 levels under 500 ms": 553 ms here against 529 ms on main in this VM. npm run build: pass.
- Requests: Ledger may hide the arena picker when the trough surface is on. Forge (pull) and Glow (drawing) read def.surface === 'trough'.
