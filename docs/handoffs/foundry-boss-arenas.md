# Hand off: foundry boss arenas
Branch feat/foundry-boss-arenas from main 1325749. GENERATOR_VERSION_V13 and GenConfig.enableBossArenas (default on) apply to Adventure guardian levels from 10 and to all their loops. V12_GEN_CONFIG turns the change off and matches main's digest 5273e626 over 300 levels. Level 5 (Brood) and every non guardian level keep their version 11 or 12 bytes.
- Rule (src/levelgen/bossArena.ts): each arena dimension grows to at least the Free Play medium size of its kind: rect, roundRect and rectObstacles to 1600 x 1000, circle to 1100, hexagon to a height of 1100. Spindle grows to large: 2080 x 1300, circle 1430, hexagon height 1430. Shape, surface (all guardian levels are flat) and obstacles stay the same, and an arena already that big is not changed. There are no membranes, because the current M1 rule allows them only on drums.
- Before and after, width x height over 200 seeds: L10 Warden 760-1400 x 760-880, now 1100-1600 x 1000-1100. L15 Spindle 760-1400 x 760-880, now 1430-2080 x 1300-1430. L20 Ember, L25 Mirage and L30 Gravitas 860-1680 x 860-1000, now 1100-1680 x 1000-1100. L35 to L50 (Bastion, Tempest, Twins, Prism) 940-1800 x 940-1100, now 1100-1800 x 1000-1100.
- Validator: J3 knows 13. A version 13 level is checked with A1 and F1 at the Free Play large scale. G1 checks that version 13 appears only on guardian levels from 10, that it matches the flags, and that the arena is at least the minimum.
