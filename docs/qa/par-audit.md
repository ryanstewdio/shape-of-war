# Par audit

Brief: docs/briefs/proctor-par-audit.md (Ryan 2026-11-11: par on level 15, Spindle, is hard to reach on Normal; check every level's par against its difficulty). Proctor, read only on src/. A proposal only: no game number changed.

## How it was measured

`node scripts/par-audit.ts` (deterministic, about 2 s; tests/qa/parAudit.test.ts checks it on one seed). Adventure levels 1 to 50, run seeds 1, 1000 and 424242, the default generator config, all four difficulties.

- Par and stars: `targetsFor` (level par times `DIFFICULTY[d].parScale`), as the HUD and results show them.
- Level time: the par model's own window (src/levelgen/pacing.ts). Non guardian: `objectiveSec` (the clock, or the expected destroy clear). Guardian: the 85 s entrance plus `BOSS_FIGHT_SEC` (180 s).
- Score available: every regular enemy that actually spawns in that time on that difficulty, as `enqueueDueWaves` plays it (`scaledCount` by `spawnRate`, repeats 0.35 s apart, and after the entrance the `WAVE_BUDGET_WITH_BOSS` accumulator: Easy 0, Normal 0.25, Hard 0.5, Insane 0.6), all killed, with geoms collected at the par model's own rate (`V10_COLLECT_RATE` 0.25), plus the guardian bonus (`bossBonus`, 25 percent of the level's Normal par on every difficulty). Guardian adds are not counted.
- Share: par over available. A player who kills everything and collects geoms like the par model reaches par when the share is below 1. Flag: a share above 1.25 x (high) or below 1 / 1.25 x (low) the median of non guardian levels on the same difficulty. Race goals are flagged the same way against the median of non guardian race goals.

## Findings

1. **Non guardian levels are steady.** Race, survive and destroy levels sit at par / available 0.50 to 0.54 on Easy, 0.49 to 0.51 on Normal, 0.41 to 0.47 on Hard and 0.35 to 0.38 on Insane, on every seed and level from 1 to 49. None is flagged. Hard and Insane par is a smaller share of the available score because parScale (1.15, 1.3) grows less than the extra spawns; that follows the R6 bot calibration (harder enemies cut what a player really kills), so it is not a defect.
2. **Score race goals on non guardian levels are steady**: 0.29 to 0.31 of available on Normal (0.6 x par), none flagged.
3. **Every guardian level is out of reach on Easy, Normal and Hard.** All 10 guardian levels (5, 10, ... 50) on all 3 seeds are flagged high on every difficulty. Par / available: Easy 2.16 to 2.91, Normal 1.73 to 2.25, Hard 1.12 to 1.27, Insane 0.81 to 0.93. Level 15 (Spindle) on Normal: 1.92 to 1.97, so even a run that kills every enemy and collects at the par rate ends at about half of par. Why:
   - **The boss calm.** Guardian par (`bossLevelPar`) still counts 180 s of full waves after the entrance. The calm now keeps 0, 25, 50 or 60 percent of them. With the calm switched off in the audit, guardian shares are 0.57, 0.56, 0.51 and 0.45, in line with other levels. The calm alone moves Normal from 0.56 to about 2.
   - **The multiplier compounds.** The waves the calm removes are the last ones, played at the highest multiplier, so keeping 25 percent of them removes far more than 75 percent of their score.
   - **The fight window.** Par assumes a 180 s fight, but the level ends when the guardian dies (Brood takes 22 to 44 s on device and on the bot). A quick kill forfeits the rest of the window: with a 60 s fight Normal reaches 2.3 to 3.2 of available (table below), and even with no calm it would be 1.9.
   - **Easy is hit hardest**: no regular enemy after the entrance, so its available score is the 85 s before the entrance plus the bonus, against a par of 0.8 x a 265 s full wave model.
4. **Guardian levels with a score race objective show an unreachable goal.** Where a guardian level draws scoreInTime (levels 5, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 50 on some seeds), the goal is 0.6 x par, so 1.07 to 1.35 of available on Normal. It is not needed to win (the guardian kill wins), but it only triggers the hold if met and the HUD shows it.

## Proposed rule (one rule, every level type)

**Par on difficulty d is s_d of the score available on d**, with s_d the non guardian medians of today: Easy 0.51, Normal 0.49, Hard 0.42, Insane 0.36. Stars stay at 1, 1.25 and 1.5 x par and race goals at 0.6 x par.

- Non guardian levels already sit on s_d: no change.
- Guardian levels: available counts full waves to the entrance, then `WAVE_BUDGET_WITH_BOSS[d]` of them over an expected fight of 90 s (not 180), plus the bonus. On Normal the bonus is 25 percent of par itself, so par = s K / (1 - 0.25 s) with K the kill score. Each par on the 500 grid.
- Expected shares (table "Proposed guardian par"): exactly s_d at a 90 s fight on every guardian level, seed and difficulty. If the guardian dies in 30 s, Normal is 0.61 to 0.92 (still reachable); if the fight lasts 180 s, 0.23 to 0.34 (easier). Level 15 on Normal, seed 1: par 2936.0k now, 268.0k proposed. Guardian par drops by about 10 x against today and its neighbours, because the score really available in a calm fight is that much smaller.

What a fix would need (Fable to rule, then owners):

- Foundry: a new generator version, `GENERATOR_VERSION_V14`, behind a new GenConfig flag, with `V13_GEN_CONFIG` frozen and a "version 13 is frozen" hash pin (as tests/levelgen/bossArenas.test.ts does for version 12). Only guardian levels change bytes and `levelHash`; every non guardian level stays byte identical. A par fight constant (90 s) separate from `BOSS_FIGHT_SEC`, and a levelgen copy of `WAVE_BUDGET_WITH_BOSS` with a test pinning it to src/game/bosses/table.ts (as `BOSS_BONUS_SHARE` is copied today).
- One par per difficulty on guardian levels: either a new optional LevelDef field appended last (for example a par per difficulty) that `targetsFor` in src/core/stars.ts reads in place of parScale, or a guardian parScale table; the audit's first try at a single table left Hard at 0.55 to 0.82, so the field is the one that meets the rule. The validator needs a rule for the new field.
- Pins likely to move: tests/levelgen/bossArenas.test.ts (default equals V12 on non guardian levels holds; guardian assertions move), tests/qa/bossScheduleGolden.test.ts (default config determinism holds), any test that reads guardian par on the default config. Older configs (V8 to V13) keep their par.
- A DECISIONS row: the par fight seconds (90) and s_d.
